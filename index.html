About
Karsh Merit Scholar · Duke '26

Engineer, product
manager, and founder —
usually all at once.

I'm a Computer Science senior at Duke University with a certificate in Innovation & Entrepreneurship from the Fuqua School of Business, graduating December 2026. I split my time between writing code, shaping product, and building the ventures that need both.

I founded CodeKenya, an end-to-end engineering bootcamp platform that reached $2K MRR, received 1000+ applications, and graduated 50+ students — 15 of whom went on to junior SWE and internship roles, with 3 launching early-stage ventures of their own.

As Product Manager at Nurshift I led a 4-person cross-functional team from concept to MVP on AI-agent-driven nurse scheduling, and secured a $10K angel round. I've shipped a MERN platform for 100K+ rugby fans at Phoenix Sports Group in Cape Town, spent this summer as a Forward Deployed Engineer at Releaf Earth (YCW19), and built the Investment Readiness Index and investor-facing MCP server for UNDP timbuktoo Africa.

I also co-founded The Real Africa to challenge global misconceptions about the continent's tech ecosystem — backed with $30K from Duke over three years.
